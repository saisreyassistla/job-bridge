Interview Tips for Job Seekers

Preparation:
Research the company before your interview. Understand their products,
culture, and recent news. Prepare questions to ask the interviewer.

Behavioral questions:
Use the STAR method — Situation, Task, Action, Result.
Prepare 8 stories covering leadership, conflict, failure, and success.

Salary negotiation:
Always negotiate. Research market rates before the interview.
Never give a number first. Let the employer make the first offer.

Day of interview:
Arrive 10 minutes early. Bring copies of your resume.
Think out loud during technical questions.

Follow up:
Send a thank you email within 24 hours.
Reference something specific from the conversation.
